# Roblox Rewind

Your Spotify Wrapped, but for Roblox spending.

See how much you've spent on Roblox, what you bought, and which games you spent the most on, all in one place.

[Install from Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/Roblox%20Rewind/cpajgohmdhddfdieelgpeifijhanbgmn)

---

### How to use it

1. Install Roblox Rewind.
2. Log in on [roblox.com](https://www.roblox.com) like you normally do.
3. Click the Roblox Rewind button in your browser.

The first time can take a few minutes. After that it's usually much faster.

---

### What you'll see

- **Your totals**: how much real money and Robux you've spent
- **Your player type**: are you a Window Shopper, a Big Spender, or something in between?
- **Your top games**: the games you spent the most Robux in
- **Fun facts**: your biggest purchase, your first item, and more
- **What it adds up to**: your spending turned into real things, like burgers or months of Netflix
- **Share image**: make a picture of your Rewind to show your friends

---

### Is it safe?

- Roblox Rewind **never asks for your password**. You log in on Roblox's own website, not in the extension.
- Your full purchase history **stays on your computer**. It isn't uploaded.
- It sends a little bit of anonymous info (like "it finished loading") to help fix bugs. It never includes your username or what you bought, and you can turn it off.

[Read the full Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Cosisety/Roblox-Rewind/blob/main/PrivacyPolicy.md)

### For parents and adults

Roblox Rewind is a simple way to see what's been spent on a Roblox account. It reads the account's transaction history directly from Roblox and does the math on your device.

Extra tools for a closer look:

- **Compare**: one year against another, or one category against another. Also has a **Monthly Digest** (last 30 days vs the 30 days before).
- **Export Data**: download the transaction data
- **Advanced Features**: choose how long data is cached, clear the cache, and open **What about my data?** to turn **Anonymous Usage Data** off

**Generate Game Code** uploads a short summary (totals, top category, top purchase; not the full history) so it can be loaded in a connected Roblox game. That game is still in beta. Codes expire after about 24 hours.
